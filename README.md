# PrepVault 🛡️

**Zero Signal. Zero Problem.**

PrepVault is a high-performance, tactical emergency readiness application built with SwiftUI. It serves as a comprehensive "Pocket Survival Kit," providing critical, life-saving information exactly when you need it most—even when you have **zero bars** of signal.

---

## 🌟 Core Philosophy

In an emergency, infrastructure can fail. PrepVault is designed for **total offline reliability**. All data and visualizations are compiled directly into the app's binary, ensuring instant access in the most remote wilderness or during city-wide power outages.

- **Offline Ready**: No network, no JSON parsing, no external dependencies.
- **OLED Optimized**: Forces deep black backgrounds to maximize battery life on modern iPhones.
- **Tactical Aesthetic**: A high-contrast "HUD" (Heads-Up Display) interface designed for clarity under stress.

---

## 🛠️ Key Features

### 🛡️ 50+ Survival Guides
Expert-sourced instructions across five critical categories:
- **Medical**: CPR, Stopping Bleeding, Treating Burns, Snake Bites, and Anaphylaxis.
- **Auto**: Tire Changes, Battery Jump-starts, and Sinking Vehicle Escape.
- **Urban**: Tornado/Earthquake protocols, Gas Leak response, and Fire Escape plans.
- **Wild**: Shelter building, Water Purification, and Bear Encounter strategies.
- **Tools**: DIY Water Filters, Emergency Knots, and Improvised Compasses.

### 🎬 Interactive Visualizations
Forget static PDFs. PrepVault uses code-drawn SwiftUI animations to demonstrate actions. These are lightweight and run perfectly on any device, showing you the exact rhythm and motion required for life-saving tasks.

### 🧠 Survival Quiz
Test your emergency instincts before the crisis hits. The built-in quiz platform tracks your best scores, longest streaks, and total attempts, reinforcing the logic behind survival tactics.

### 📍 Command Center
- **Pinning**: Keep your most critical guides (like CPR) at the top of your dashboard.
- **Search**: Instant, indexed search across all instruction steps.
- **Voice Guidance**: Integrated Text-to-Speech allows for hands-free instructions when your hands are busy saving lives.

---

## 💻 Technical Architecture

- **Language**: Swift 6
- **UI Framework**: SwiftUI
- **Project Format**: Swift Playgrounds (.swiftpm)
- **Audio Steering**: AVFoundation for clear, high-quality instruction playback.
- **Design System**: A custom-built tactical OS using a centralized design token system for visual consistency.
